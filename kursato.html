The art of Japanese home brewing

Kurasu began as an online homeware venture and grew into an internationally recognised leader in Japanese coffee brewing equipment. Our mission is to introduce the world to Japanese coffee culture.

We don’t just sell equipment. We teach the Japanese way of brewing at home, the culture behind it, and the craft of coffee itself. Everything we use in our cafés is available for you to recreate at home.
